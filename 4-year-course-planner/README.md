# 4-Year Course Planner

A single-page HTML tool for students to plan their high school career.

## What it does
1. Students mark the classes they have **already taken** in previous years.
2. Students select the classes they plan to take **next school year**.
3. The planner tracks **graduation requirements** and shows which ones are met and which are still needed.
4. Students can **export a form** listing their completed/planned classes and the requirements they have hit.
