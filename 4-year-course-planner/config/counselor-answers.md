## Rules to build

1. Four focus areas: AP, Fine Arts & Humanities, CCTE, Math & Science. Student picks one (default
   suggested at 9th grade); can change; tool notes if another area is closer to complete.
2. **AP:** any 3 courses with Level = AP. Can also satisfy graduation requirements. No exam needed.
3. **CCTE:** 3 courses forming one pathway progression (I -> II -> III, or the pathway's sequence).
   Warn when no pathway is started by the end of 10th grade.
4. **Fine Arts & Humanities:** any mix of fine arts, world language beyond the 2 required, and
   humanities electives (e.g. Psychology, Sociology). A course used for a graduation requirement
   does NOT also count here (fine arts must be beyond the 1 required credit).
5. **Math & Science:** default (unanswered): credits beyond the 4 math / 3 science requirements.
6. Credits beyond 3 in the focus count as general electives.
7. Total credits for graduation = **22** (see open item below).
