Classes Taken

Add classes you have already completed. Find the course, choose the year you took it, and enter your Fall Semester Grade and Spring Semester Grade. Took Algebra I, Geometry, or a World Language in middle school? Choose Middle School as the year.

Already have last year's plan? If you saved a PDF from this planner last year, go to the Save / Load tab and choose Import a PDF. Your completed classes will load in automatically, so you do not have to add them one by one. The PDF must have been created by this same planner - a school transcript or any other PDF will not work.

Requirement Tracker

What you have earned, what is planned, and what is still needed. Click a requirement to see the exact classes being applied to it.

Save / Load Your Plan

You will use this planner again next year. Export your plan as a PDF now and save the file. Next year, open the planner, come back to this tab, and import that PDF. You will pick up exactly where you left off, and every class you completed this year will already be filled in for you.

Save your plan (export a PDF)

This creates a PDF that lists every class you have completed and planned, along with your progress toward each graduation requirement. The PDF also stores your plan inside the file so it can be loaded back next year.

After it downloads, keep it somewhere safe - email it to yourself, save it to your school drive, or put it in a folder you will find again next year. It is not saved anywhere automatically.

Load last year's plan (import a PDF)

Used this planner in a previous year? Choose the PDF it made for you and everything loads back in: your completed classes, your current status, your graduating class, and any classes you had planned.

It must be a PDF that this planner created. A school transcript or any other PDF will not work.
