CLASSROOM APP - how to use

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere you can write files (Documents or Desktop is fine).
   Do not run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click "Start App.bat". A black window opens (leave it open) and your browser
   opens to  http://127.0.0.1:3000
3. To stop the app, close the black window.

Everything needed is inside this folder, including its own copy of Node.
Nothing is installed and nothing is downloaded.

YOUR DATA
  The "data" folder (created on first run) holds your students, scores and settings.
  It stays on this computer. Back it up by copying that folder.

UPDATING TO A NEW VERSION
  Unzip the new version into a NEW folder, then copy your old "data" folder into it.
  (Or unzip over the old folder: the zip never contains a "data" folder, so yours is kept.)

The app only answers to this computer. Other devices on the network cannot open it.
