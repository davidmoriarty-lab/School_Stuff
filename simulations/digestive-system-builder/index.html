Nutrient values are in Calories; water is in grams. Initial = In the gut now + Absorbed (after digestion converts one form into another).

How it works

  1. Build. Drag parts from the trays onto the grid; they snap to the squares. You can also click a part and then click a square. Drag a part off the grid (or double-click it) to put it back.
  2. Connect the tube. Food enters the mouth and moves left to right. Two organs are joined when the open end of one sits against the open end of the next.
  3. Attach the helpers. A gland, group of cells or vessel works on the organ it is touching. A small connector appears when it is attached.
  4. Feed it. Choose a food and press Play. Watch the Analysis table: digestion converts nutrients into smaller units, and absorption moves them out of the gut.
  5. Read the result. At the end you get a report of what went wrong and why. Fix the build and try again, then try other foods.

Keyboard: choose a part with Tab and Enter, move it with the arrow keys, press Enter to place it, Esc to cancel.
