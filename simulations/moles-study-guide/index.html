The color key

The same four colors mean the same thing on every page. Each also has its own underline style, so you can tell them apart even without color.

givenThe number and unit you start with.
solid underline
findWhat the question asks for, and the final answer.
double underline
looked upA number you look up or a conversion factor: atomic masses, molar mass, 6.022 × 10²³, 22.4 L/mol.
dotted underline
countA count: a subscript, the number of atoms, a multiplier.
dashed underline
canceled unitA unit that cancels because it is on the top of one fraction and the bottom of the next. The unit left over should be the one you want.
